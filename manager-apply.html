About the role

SomiSomi is a Korean-Japanese soft serve and taiyaki shop with two locations — Shenandoah (The Woodlands) and West Houston. This role will be very hands on. We are NOT looking for someone who just shows up and tells people what to do but actually picks up a broom, washes dishes, mops, cleans the restroom etc. Small spaces, focused menu, teams of 13–15 part-timers per location.

The Woodlands runs at a high standard. West Houston is being brought up to match — directors are hands-on through that process, but maintaining the standard across both stores is your responsibility.

You'll split your time evenly between both stores. Each store has its own team. Your job is to make sure both run the way they're supposed to — whether you're there or not.

This is not a clock-in, clock-out job. If something goes wrong on your day off, you're the one who gets the call. If a team member doesn't show up, you find the solution. If a store isn't at standard, that's on you.

If that doesn't sound like the role for you, please don't apply. If it sounds exactly like how you already operate, we'd love to meet you.
